# Changelog for @threepipe/plugin-tweakpane-editor

All notable changes to this plugin will be documented in this file.

[//]: # (The format is based on [Keep a Changelog]&#40;https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/&#41;, and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning]&#40;https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html&#41;.)

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `UiConfigForm`: the `bar` and `stack` layouts (`layout` option, `form.layout`, type `UiConfigFormLayout`) draw the configs as a toolbar: buttons, checkboxes as toggle buttons (`aria-pressed`), dropdowns as selects, and the other fields (slider, number, colour, vector, text) as a button with the value that opens the field in a popover; folders are groups with a line between them. The colours follow the Tweakpane theme of the page (light, or dark with the system, without one). Tooltips in the editor look show the label of an icon-only button and the key of `shortcutOf(config)` (`UiConfigForm.Tooltips`). In a bar on a narrow screen (640 px), buttons with an icon show the icon only. A monitor is a read-only row with its value; in a stack, a button shows its key at the end of its row, and a folder shows its name as a small heading. Example: `ui-config-form` (a bar under the form); the examples use the stack in their card (`createExampleCard`).
- `UiConfigForm`: the `icon` of a config (uiconfig.js 0.4.2): SVG markup, an image URL, or the name of a Lucide icon of the form (`lucideIcons`, `lucideIcon(name)`, a part of Lucide, ISC License). An `icon` or a `label` that is a function is drawn again on `refresh()`.
- `UiConfigForm`: an async button is disabled, with a spinner, until its promise settles (all layouts); an error of a button goes to `onError` (default `console.error`) and enables it again, it is no longer an unhandled rejection.

- The keys of the editor are actions of the `CommandsPlugin` of threepipe when the viewer has one (added before or after the editor), so a toolbar, a menu or a command palette lists and runs them: `editorFrame.toggleTheme` (Shift+T) and `editorFrame.cycleModeBar` (Ctrl+\\ / Cmd+\\) in `EditorFrame.actions`, `editorCamera.toggleEditing` (the key of `hotkey`) in `EditorCameraPlugin.actions`. Without the plugin, the editor runs the same keys itself (`bindActions` of threepipe), with the same behaviour as before. `EditorFrameOptions.viewer` gives the frame its viewer (`TweakpaneEditorPlugin` passes it)
- `EditorCameraPlugin.shortcut`: the key of the Edit/View switch now, the one that a `CommandsPlugin` sets (`setShortcut`) or else `hotkey`; the start view pill shows it, and `hotkeyChange` also tells a change of the actions of the `CommandsPlugin`. The gizmo Space key turns a gizmo on again while it is off.
- Toasts: the `CommandsPlugin` of threepipe shows the `error` and the `warn` of each action run in a toast of the viewer dialog (`viewer.dialog.toast`); the editor puts it at the top centre of the canvas, under the camera pill (viewer UI layer, `z-index` 130), with a lighter chip in a dark theme (the CSS variables `--dialog-toast-top`, `--dialog-toast-z`, `--dialog-toast-bg`, `--dialog-toast-shadow`). The Escape that closes the camera card or the layout menu is used there: it does not also clear the selection.
- `TweakpaneEditorPlugin.preventIosInputZoom` (on by default): on iOS and iPadOS, the page does not zoom into a focused text field with text under 16 px (iOS does not zoom back out), with `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta tag while the plugin is added; a pinch zoom still works in Safari (an in-app browser or a web app on the home screen blocks it: turn the setting off there), other systems do not change. The helpers `preventIosInputZoom()` and `isIOS()` are exported.
- Add `LayersPanelPlugin`: the objects of the scene (`scene.modelRoot`) as a tree, in place of the `HierarchyUiPlugin`. A click selects in the `PickingPlugin` (Ctrl, Cmd or Shift + click adds or takes out); a selection in the viewport marks its rows, opens its groups and scrolls to it (`revealSelection`). The arrow of a row opens or closes it (Alt: all the levels; `setExpanded`, `isExpanded`, `collapseAll`). The scene and the default camera are the first rows (`sceneRows`), dimmer, and a search finds them by name; meshes, lines, points, cameras and lights show no children (`leafTypes`, `showLeafChildren`); a double-click focuses a row (`doubleClickFocus`), and a click can unselect a selected row (`clickToUnselect`). Tabs for the materials, textures and geometries that the objects use (`objectsTab`, `materialsTab`, `texturesTab`, `geometryTab`, all on; one tab shows no tab bar; `tabs`, `tab`, `setTab`): an asset row has a swatch, a picture or an icon, its size or vertices and its users, which its arrow lists; a click selects the asset, a double-click selects its users, a double-click on the name or F2 renames it (`renameAsset`), a hover outlines its users. An open row shows its first `maxChildren` children (5), then a "… N more" row that shows all of them (`showAllChildren`); the top level, a search and the selection always show all. A hovered row outlines its object with the `SelectionOutlinePlugin` (`viewportHover`) when the pointer rests on it (`LayersPanelPlugin.HoverDelay`, 80 ms): one more frame, no restart of the progressive render; the object hovered in the viewport is marked in the list. The eye hides or shows, the lock locks or unlocks, for the whole selection when the row is in it (`setVisible`, `setLocked`, undo steps); a hidden or locked parent dims the buttons of its children. A locked object has `userData.userSelectable` set to `false`, the rule of the `PickingPlugin`, so it and its parts are not picked in the viewport; it can still be selected in the list. An unlock gives back the value from before the lock. A double-click on a name, or F2, renames (`renameObject`, `startRename`; an undo step). In the focused list, Up and Down select (Shift adds), Right opens or goes in, Left closes or goes to the parent. A search field filters by name (a × button clears it). Lights and cameras have the icon and the colour of their kind (`widgetKinds`). A virtual list: only the rows in view are in the DOM. It goes in the `container` given to the constructor, else the app places `element`. The plugin does not depend on the editor: in the page frame, the app adds it as a panel, `TweakpaneEditorPlugin.addPanel('layers', layers.element, {...LayersPanelPlugin.AreaEntry, area: 'left'})` (a name, an icon and a colour for the Layers entry in the mode bar), and the `enabledChange` event (`{enabled}`) tells it when the panel is turned on or off. Its UI folder, `Layers Panel`, has the settings. Example `layers-panel-plugin`; the tweakpane-editor example shows it in place of the `HierarchyUiPlugin`, in the left area (`?layers=bottom`: in the bottom strip) (its `?areas` sample is now only in the bottom strip).
- `NotesPlugin`: a list of notes, each kept in a store: `file` (serialized, so saved in the viewer config and in an exported .glb), `browser` (`localStorage`, `BrowserNotesStore`), or a store of the app (`NotesStore`, `addStore`; `HttpNotesStore` for a server). Changes are saved per store 600 ms after the last one; notes made while a store loads are kept. Events `notesChange` (with the note, or the notes that `clear` or a replaced store removed), `storeStatusChange` and `defaultStoreChange`; `contextLine()` gives the time, the context and the selected names. Its UI folder, `Notes`, has the settings.
- `StickyNotesUiPlugin`: the notes as post-its over the canvas, in the `board` layout (a post-it per note; new ones, and "Arrange", inside the canvas and above the dock, also on a phone) or the `stack` layout (a pile that goes to the left and up; a click brings a post-it up), or `minimized` (off the canvas; one button with the number of notes brings back the layout from before). The post-its start minimized on each page load (the second constructor argument, `startMinimized`, default `true`). The buttons are icon buttons with tooltips (the `editor` theme): add a note at once; the store of new notes (its icon; a click opens a menu of the stores); the icon of a note's store (the same menu moves the note); add the time and the selection, copy the text (a check and "Copied" for a moment) and delete per note (the text is 16 px on a touch screen, so a phone does not zoom in on it); post-its are dragged by their bar and sized from their corner, and glide between layouts. The layout, the colour, the places, the sizes and the order are kept in `localStorage`. Its UI folder, `Sticky Notes`, has the settings; `dock` holds its buttons. The post-its show above the loading screen, and their menus with the popups of the page. The `notes-plugin` example and the Extras mode of the tweakpane editor example show both.
- `StickyNotesUiPlugin`: the dock drags by a handle at its left (or its free part; the buttons still click) and stays inside the canvas, also on a resize; a double click on the handle, or Reset places, puts it back in its corner. Its place is kept in `localStorage` with the other places. "Add a note" opens the post-it by the dock, above it (below it when the dock is at the top), inside the canvas, instead of at the top left of the canvas over the toolbar and the panels; notes added in code still go in the columns, and the stack layout is unchanged. The notes and the open perf card (`PerfPanelPlugin`) stack by the last press (`raiseOnPress` of threepipe): a press on a post-it or the dock brings the notes forward, opening the perf card brings it forward.
- Add `TweakpaneEditorPlugin.layout`: the page layout, `titlebar`, `statusbar`, `floating` or `classic`. The first three put the editor in a page frame (class `EditorFrame`) with the modes as coloured icons on the right (`EditorModeBar`; its bottom button and Ctrl+\\ / Cmd+\\ step through the names and the panel, the icons and the panel, the icons only (`EditorModeBar.cycle()`); a click on the open mode hides the panel) and Shift+T for a light or dark theme. The light or dark button and the layout button are at the bottom of the mode bar, above the names toggle, at the same place in each layout; the status bar has the logo and the app name, like the title bar. `classic` is the old page, with the mode tabs at the bottom. The layout picked in the frame's layout menu or in the plugin's UI is kept in `localStorage` (`TweakpaneEditorPlugin.LayoutStorageKey`; a layout set from code is not); `TweakpaneEditorPlugin.DefaultLayout` sets the layout when none is stored (set both before the plugin is made), and `frameOptions` the app name and theme pairs. `TweakpaneEditorPlugin.documentName` is the name in the bar (the open file or project), set by the app; a click on it in the bar renames it, and each change sends `documentNameChange`. The tweakpane-editor example sets it from the first loaded model or viewer config file, and names the exported file with it. The names setting that the person picks in the mode bar is kept too (`EditorModeBar.StorageKey`; one set from code is not); `EditorModeBar.onExpandedChange` is called when it changes. In the tweakpane-editor example: `?layout=titlebar|statusbar|floating|classic`.
- The page frame on a phone: a window under 920 px wide keeps the chosen layout (its bars and the mode bar; floating has no gap there) instead of the classic page, so the Page layout menu and dropdown show the layout on screen. In portrait, the canvas is on top at full width (`--lay-split`, 0.45 of the height), and the panel and the mode bar are below it, side by side with the same height; a mode bar taller than its room scrolls. When the panel is hidden, the mode bar is a row at the bottom and the canvas takes the rest. In landscape, they are side by side as on a desktop. The mode names are hidden at the first visit in a narrow window (`EditorModeBar.WideQuery`).
- The page frame has three fixed places (areas, `EditorFrameArea`): the left sidebar, the bottom strip below the canvas, and the top of the right sidebar. An app adds panels to them: `TweakpaneEditorPlugin.addPanel(id, element, {name, icon, color, area})` (and `EditorFrame.addPanel`; type `EditorFramePanelOptions`), `removePanel(id)`; the plugin adds the panels again when it makes the frame again. An empty place takes no room, so a page without panels looks the same as before. A shown area is `--lay-left-area-size` wide (240 px) or `--lay-bottom-area-size` tall (160 px); in the floating layout it is a card over the canvas. The body has the class `lay-has-left` or `lay-has-bottom`, and the CSS variables `--lay-left-area`, `--lay-right-area` and `--lay-bottom-area` (and `--lay-left-inset`, `--lay-bottom-inset` in the floating layout) give the room of each area: the perf pill, the bar of the sticky notes and the tool bar keep out of the cards. The mode bar has an entry for each panel, after the modes (its name, icon and colour, type `EditorFrameAreaOptions`): a tap shows or hides the panel (`EditorFrame.setPanelHidden`, `isPanelHidden`; hiding the last panel of a place collapses the place, as does moving or removing it when only hidden panels stay), and a right-click, or a long press on a touch screen (`EditorFrame.LongPressTime`), opens a menu of the places that moves it (`EditorFrame.movePanel`, `panelArea`, `panelIds`; in a narrow window the new place shows). The place, the place in the stack and the hidden state that the person picks are kept by panel id. A removed panel takes the tooltip of its entry out of the mode bar (`EditorModeBar.removeTooltip(button)`, the pair of `addTooltip`). A narrow window has no room for the areas next to the canvas: the place of the panel (below the canvas on a phone) shows one view at a time, a mode's panel or an area picked with its entry (`EditorFrame.setStackedArea(area)`, `stackedArea`, the body class `lay-stacked-left` or `lay-stacked-bottom`, kept in `localStorage`); a tap on the one shown hides it. The layout changes at the widths of `EditorFrame.Breakpoints` (`{stack: 920, singleView: 700}`): the body classes `lay-side`, `lay-stack` and `lay-single` replace the media queries of the frame, and a narrow landscape window (under `singleView`) also shows the areas one at a time; areas at the size of the CSS shrink to leave room for the canvas.
- The left area, the bottom area and the panel of the page frame change size with a drag of their inner edge (`EditorFrame.setAreaSize(area, px)`, between `EditorFrame.AreaLimits`), and collapse with a drag to the side (`setAreaCollapsed(area, collapsed)`, `isAreaCollapsed(area)`; the panel through the mode bar). A double-click on an edge sets the default size or opens a collapsed area. The sizes and the collapsed left and bottom areas that the person picks are kept in `localStorage` (`EditorFrame.AreaStorageKey`). Type `EditorFrameSizedArea`.
- The floating cards of the page frame move and take a part of their side: a drag of the grip of a card (a strip of dots in the gap on the side of the window, blue on hover) moves it away from its side and along it, and the ends of a card along its side resize it (`EditorFrame.setAreaOffset(area, px)`, `setAreaSpan(area, {start, length})`, `areaOffset`, `areaSpan`, `MinCardLength`; type `EditorFrameAreaSpan`). The panel and the mode bar are one card. After a drag, an end of a card that touches the end of its side stays there, so the card grows with its side. The offset and the size share the room for the canvas; both are kept with the sizes. The line of an edge stops at the round corners of a floating card. Example `editor-frame-areas` (sample content in the areas; it replaces `?areas` of the tweakpane-editor example).
- The panels of a place are a stack: they split the place, with a line between two that moves the split (a gap with drag dots in the floating layout, where each panel is a card), at least `EditorFrame.MinSectionSize`; a double-click makes them equal; the sizes are kept by panel id. The floating layout has a head at the top of the left column with the logo, the app name and the file name (`.lay-head`, `--lay-head`), as wide as the left card and moved with it.
- The right place of the page frame: its panels are at the top of the panel of the Tweakpane UI, above the settings of the mode, with a line between them that a drag moves (two cards with a gap and drag dots in the floating layout; the part is kept). While it shows, a click on the open mode hides the settings of the mode (`EditorFrame.setModeSettingsHidden`, `modeSettingsHidden`; body classes `lay-right-shown`, `lay-mode-hidden`) and the right place takes the panel; a click on a mode, or `setSelectedMode`, shows them again. `TweakpaneEditorPlugin` sends `frameChange` (`{frame}`) when it makes or removes the frame, so an app sets the state of a new frame again. The CSS of the frame for the panel styles only the main pane of the Tweakpane UI (a direct child of its container), not a Tweakpane UI in a panel of the right place.
- `TweakpaneEditorPlugin` has a UI (`Editor` folder: Page layout, Mode names), shown in the Viewer mode of the tweakpane-editor example, also in the classic layout.
- Add `EditorCameraPlugin`: the editor's own camera (Edit, 1:1 with the pointer, no limits, never saved) and the scene's start view (View, through `previewCamera`, a copy of the default camera with the visitor's controls). `syncStartView` (on by default) saves each move in View as it happens (one undo step per move; with orbit controls, only a user gesture is a move, not a clamp of the orbit limits or a camera animation of another plugin); with it off, a move in View is a preview: `saveStartView()` keeps it, `resetStartView()` goes back; an export in View always has the saved start view. `setStartViewFromEditor()` (the view that is seen: the editor view in Edit, the preview in View) with undo, `smooth`. The C key switches Edit and View (`hotkey`). Events `editingChange`, `startViewMovedChange` (`startViewMoved`), `startViewChange`, `syncStartViewChange` and `hotkeyChange`; `showsStartView`. In the `TweakpaneEditorPlugin`, while the plugin is added: a pill on the canvas in the style of the perf pill names the camera ("Editor camera" or "Scene camera", with a grey, blue or green camera icon and the key of `hotkey`; a frame in View; hidden while the plugin is disabled) and opens a card with a picture of the saved start view, a Sync toggle, an Edit / View toggle and the start view actions (Set as start view; Save and Reset after a move in View); `setupEditorCameraUi()` for other apps (it returns a function that removes the UI). Example `editor-camera-plugin`; also in the `tweakpane-editor` example (Viewer tab).
- Add `SelectionOutlinePlugin`: the outline of the selected and hovered objects in the render pipeline (a mask pass and a screen pass extension), with lines between parts, a dashed x-ray outline, fill and dim; follows the `PickingPlugin` or takes `selected` and `hovered`; the mask follows the material alpha. `antialias` (off by default): a smooth outer edge of about 1 px, the same width at each angle, and an inner edge that follows the anti-aliased edge of the object instead of the pixel steps of the mask; off gives the outline without it, with fewer mask reads. A selection or hover change renders one frame, without a progressive restart. The mask is drawn again only after a change (selection, hover, settings, a render reset, another camera or a resize), not in each frame of the progressive render; its target is made at the first selection or hover. The selection widget (box) of the `PickingPlugin` is off while the plugin outlines the selection, as with `OutlinePlugin` (a selection without an outline keeps it: a camera, a light, a group without meshes, a line). Also `SelectionMaskPass`. Example `selection-outline-plugin`; also in the Interaction mode of the tweakpane-editor example, next to `OutlinePlugin` (its UI folder is `Selection Outline (Editor)`). In that example `OutlinePlugin` is now off by default, so a selection has one outline; turn either one on or off in the Interaction mode.
- Add `InspectorPlugin`: the settings of the selection of the `PickingPlugin`, like the inspector of Unity. A head with the name, tabs for the parts (Object, Material, Geometry; Texture for a selected texture; `tab`, `setTab`; a pick of another object keeps the open tab), and the uiConfig of the open tab in a second Tweakpane renderer with the theme of the page. It turns off `PickingPlugin.selectionUi` while it is in the page (out of it, as in the classic layout, the Picker folder shows the selection), and shows the actions of the PickingPlugin itself (Focus and Select Parent on the Object tab, Remove Material(s) and New … Material on the Material tab). The Object tab has a card for each material of the selected mesh (a small preview, `InspectorPlugin.CardPreviewSize`, that follows its material and the lighting of the viewer, the name and the type); a click opens the Material tab on it. The Material tab has a large live material preview from the `MaterialPreviewPlugin` (`preview`, also a Preview head that folds it; `previewShape`: sphere, cube or this mesh; drag to turn, wheel to zoom, double-click to reset; `InspectorPlugin.PreviewSize`) and a list of the materials of a mesh with more than one; the preview follows the changes of the material, of the environment map and the tone mapping of the viewer, and of the selected geometry in place (`geometryUpdate`, for the This mesh shape). A search field filters the settings by name and the folders by title (the folders open while it has text; Escape or the × button clears it). The tab bar and the search field are the ones of the Layers panel (the same sizes and font). A right-click on a setting (a long press on a touch screen, `InspectorPlugin.LongPressTime`) offers Reset to default when the value is not the default of its class (a new object of the same class), with undo; not on a disabled or read-only setting, nor for an object value with no `clone`. `hasSelection`, events `selectionChange` and `enabledChange`, `InspectorPlugin.AreaEntry`. The app places `element`: a panel of the page frame (`TweakpaneEditorPlugin.addPanel`), swapped in the right panel, above the mode settings, or in another place.
- The tweakpane-editor example shows the inspector as a panel of the page frame: `?inspector=swap` (the default: in the right place, in place of the mode settings while something is selected; only while the inspector is in the right place, so the person can move it), `right` (above the mode settings) or `left` (under the layers).
- Add `UiConfigForm`: a small plain-DOM form for uiconfig.js configs (number with scrub, slider, checkbox, dropdown, vector, color, text, button, monitor, folders), for popups where a Tweakpane panel is too large. Optional undo (quick edits of one field are one step), `onChange` of the config and its folders, styles that follow the Tweakpane theme variables. Example `ui-config-form`.

### Changed

- `UiConfigForm`: a given `label` is shown as it is (`camera.fitObject`); only a label from the key of the property gets spaces (`roughnessMap`: `roughness Map`).
- The start view pill shows the key of `EditorCameraPlugin.hotkey` with `formatShortcut` of threepipe: `KeyC` is C as before, `Escape` is Esc, `Backslash` is \\
- Breaking: `TweakpaneEditorPlugin` opens the editor in the title bar frame by default (`TweakpaneEditorPlugin.DefaultLayout = 'titlebar'`), with the modes as icons on the right instead of the tabs at the bottom. Apps keep the old page with `TweakpaneEditorPlugin.DefaultLayout = 'classic'` (before the plugin is made; a layout that the person picks is still kept) or `editor.layout = 'classic'`.
- On a phone, the bottom tool bar fits in one row (smaller gaps and side padding; a narrower canvas scrolls it sideways instead of wrapping). In the page frame, the tool bar has icon buttons with the hover and on states of the frame's bars and mode bar, and no dividers: 28 px buttons with 16 px icons, as in the title bar (the status bar buttons are 22 px, the mode icons 20 px). The tooltip of the layout button hides while its menu is open, so it does not cover the first item (also in the mode bar of the floating layout). The perf pill (`PerfPanelPlugin`, bottom left) sits at the bottom left in the frame, in the middle of the tool bar's height (its own size, 22 px, in a window 920 px wide or more; 32 px like the tool bar in a narrower one); the tool bar leaves room for it in every layout. On a touch screen, the editor's tooltips show on a long press only (a tap no longer leaves one open until the next tap). The panel scrolls with a finger (it takes the touches: iOS does not scroll a container with `pointer-events: none`) and stops at its ends without moving the page; in the classic layout it is as tall as its content, up to the room above the tool bar.
- The editor's tooltips (the bottom button bar, the fullscreen button, the mode bar, the frame buttons, the camera pill and the sticky notes) share one tippy theme, `editor`: a small dark chip with its arrow, lighter in the dark themes. Before, the button bar tooltips took the panel colours, with a dark arrow in the light themes. The camera pill and the sticky notes add the tippy.js CSS and the theme themselves (once per page), so they also work without `TweakpaneEditorPlugin`.
- treejs comes from npm, `@repalash/treejs` 2.0.0 (the same code), instead of a git URL, which npm 12 refuses by default. `HierarchyUiPlugin.treeView` is typed `any`: treejs is bundled, so its type could not resolve in consumer declarations.

### Deprecated

- `HierarchyUiPlugin`: use `LayersPanelPlugin`. It stays, with no new features.

### Removed

- The webgi logo at the bottom left of the canvas (`#webgi-logo`, added by `TweakpaneEditorPlugin`): the image https://static.webgi.xyz/logo.svg with the tooltip "Powered by Threepipe SDK", that opened https://webgi.dev in a new tab, and its URL parameters `logo-img` (another image) and `logo-link` (another link). The perf pill and the post-its dock are at the bottom left now.
