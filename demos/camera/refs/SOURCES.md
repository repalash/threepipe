## Readings from the photographs (not published; ± stated)

Pixel coordinates are on the files in this folder. Scale from the published width (150.7 mm) across
the body silhouette, excluding strap lugs.

- `body-rear.jpg`: body x 243–1746 px → 0.1003 mm/px. Eyepiece and hot-shoe centre 15.5 mm left
  of the body centre (seen from behind), i.e. the optical axis is offset to the non-grip side.
  LCD glass 72.5 × 49.8 mm, its centre 20.2 mm left of centre, 41 mm above the base. Quick-control
  dial Ø30 mm at 36.4 mm right of centre, 27 mm up. (±1 mm.)
- `body-mount.jpg`: body x 230–1599 px (shoe top y 89, base y 1151) → 0.1102 mm/px; height/width
  0.776 vs the published 0.772. The photograph is yawed a few degrees (the +X side face and its
  port covers are visible), so the mount centre reads 9.6 mm off centre against 15.5 mm from the
  rear view. The mount ring is an ellipse 580 × 538 px: the lens is tilted ~22° to the camera, so
  vertical readings are foreshortened ~7 %. Lens axis 48 ± 2 mm above the base.
- `body-side.jpg`: wide lens, on a battery grip; used only for the depth order of the mount
  flange, the front face beside the mount (≈15 mm behind the flange), the prism front and the rear.
