N° 77 boulevard de Sébastopol, Paris - Paul Mesnard, architect, published by César Daly in 1870 - modelled to scale from the engraved elevation and plans, entirely through @threepipe/plugin-modelling's JSON commands.

Reference and result

Every dimension comes from Daly's plates, read in pixels and converted with the plates' own scale bars; the street photographs show what the drawings do not - how the balconies and the mansard turn a cut corner, the zinc, the stacks.

The build 12 captures, in order

The plan outline (48 vertices: corners and window jambs) is extruded once per storey line; openings are inset, recessed and glazed; bands are pushed out of the wall by region inset; the mansard is the top face inset with a depth equal to its width.

The plan's own scale bar (35.57 px/m) disagrees with the elevation by 4.2 %: it would put the six boulevard bays 2.47 m apart against the elevation's 2.371 m. The elevation is twice the scale and puts the cornice on the 20 m of the 1859 decree, so the plan is calibrated on the same six bays instead (37.07 px/m). Even so, the plan draws the party wall 0.58 m beyond the elevation's edge; the elevation stops at the wall's axis and the plan draws all of it, which explains about half of that. The model follows the elevation.

Boundary edges are expected and accounted for: the façade's are exactly the rims of the holes the glass and the doors were separated from (one loop per pane; together the three objects are closed - see no per-face materials), the dormers' are their panes, the roof's is its eave, sitting on the wall, and the road is a plane. Nothing is non-manifold.

What the API lacked

  • Curve array keeps Blender's Z-up frames in a Y-up scene
    Railings on a ground-plane path came out upside down on some runs; built Z-up and stood up by hand.
  • No per-face material slots
    Glass had to be separated from the stone, leaving a hole per window in the façade mesh.
  • Individual inset silently ignores outset
    Balcony slabs grow outward only with a negative thickness, which nothing tells you.
  • Session harness waited 30 s, not 90 s, for the page (fixed here)
    On a loaded machine every session died before the build began.
  • Edges could not be addressed (fixed on modelling-editor, cherry-picked)
    Bevelling the cornice soffit and the balcony noses needs edge indices; `inspect` now lists them.
  • Sweep profile orientation (open, from the Eiffel build)
    Avoided: mouldings are made by region inset rather than swept profiles.
  • No booleans yet (waits on manifold-3d)
    Shop fronts and dormer cheeks would be cut cleanly into the wall and the roof; here the dormers simply sit in the brisis.
