# Reference images

All files here were downloaded from the sources below and are used unmodified apart from
re-encoding (and, for the photographs, downscaling). The Daly plates keep their original pixel size,
so the pixel coordinates quoted in `build.mjs` and `../README` sections refer to these files.

## The building: Maison à loyer, N° 77 boulevard de Sébastopol, Paris (Paul Mesnard, architect)

From César Daly (ed.), *L'architecture privée au XIXe siècle sous Napoléon III. Nouvelles maisons de
Paris et des environs*, Série 1, tome 2: *Maisons à loyer*, Paris: Ducher, 1870, Exemple M2.
Digitised by Heidelberg University Library, shelfmark T 2292::1,2,
DOI [10.11588/diglit.18393](https://doi.org/10.11588/diglit.18393).

Licence: the manifest declares the [Public Domain Mark 1.0](http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/)
(the plates were engraved before 1870). Attribution kept as a courtesy: "Universitätsbibliothek

## Text sources

- Décret du 27 juillet 1859 (Paris building heights): on streets wider than 20 m the façade may reach
  20 m, and the roof stays inside a 45° line from the eaves. Summarised in FR-Wikipedia,
  [Règlements d'urbanisme de Paris](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%A8glements_d%27urbanisme_de_Paris).
  The boulevard de Sébastopol is 30 m wide, so both limits apply to N° 77.
