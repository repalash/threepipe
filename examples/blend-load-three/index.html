Blend Load

plain three.js

The same .blend loader in a plain three.js page, without threepipe: BlendLoader from @threepipe/plugin-blend-importer/three, with three r170 from a CDN. Pick a scene, orbit around, or drop your own .blend.

import {BlendLoader} from '@threepipe/plugin-blend-importer/three'

const {scene: model} = await new BlendLoader().loadAsync(url)
scene.add(model)
